# Code of conduct

Project participation must be respectful, technically honest, and safe.

Acceptable behavior includes constructive review, evidence-backed disagreement,
respect for privacy and licensing, and acknowledgement of product limitations.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private
source or vulnerability details, plagiarism, and knowingly misleading test or
provenance claims are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and participation that
violate these expectations. Security or privacy matters should use the private
process in `SECURITY.md`, not a public conduct thread. This policy does not create
a service-level response commitment.
